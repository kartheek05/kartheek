My First Web Page

A webpage is a digital document on the World Wide Web, written in HTML, that can display text, images, and other media. It's designed to be viewed in a web browser and can be linked to other webpages.

Uses of Web Pages

Webpages serve as accessible platforms to present information, share content, and facilitate online interaction for individuals,businesses,and organizations worldwide. They enable communication,commerce,education and entertainment,acting as a global digital presence.
